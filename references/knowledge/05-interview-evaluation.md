# Interview evaluation

Interview defense starts from the sentence most likely to break under questioning.
Prioritize strong ownership, architecture decisions, quantified results, production
claims and awards. Match the drill to the JD and round, not a random question bank.

Inspect technical depth, ownership, evidence, decision quality, alternatives, failures
and communication. A polished answer is insufficient when its measurement or author
boundary is missing. AI assistance should be explained as actual tool use and validation.

Feedback that architecture was discussed supports the topic's relevance in that
interview. It does not prove the candidate failed, that all future interviews will
weight it equally, or that preparation caused success. Preserve explicit feedback,
candidate interpretation and unknown selection criteria separately.
