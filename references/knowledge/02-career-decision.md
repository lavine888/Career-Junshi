# Career decision

Choose the next reversible move that improves the user's actual goal under their
constraints. First locate Direction / Evidence / Positioning / Interview / Market /
Timing; do not assume the bottleneck is wording.

For a role: inspect responsibilities, evidence fit, learning, ownership, direction,
opportunity cost and the cost of closing the gap. Hard constraints precede scoring.
Differentiate requirements from “nice to have”. Recommend apply / bounded test /
defer with a specific reason; do not assign a universal company ranking.

For an uncertain direction, form two hypotheses from the strongest real projects.
Test against 5 comparable current JDs and a small set of applications or conversations.
Choose one primary hypothesis and at most one side hypothesis when evidence permits.
If no evidence is supplied, collecting and auditing three projects is the next move;
it is not grounds to declare any career unsuitable.

KEEP: evidence and repeated feedback support the direction. REFINE: role is plausible
but recurring specific gap can be closed cheaply. PIVOT: repeated comparable feedback,
hard constraints or opportunity costs undermine the hypothesis. One rejection or
one success does not establish a pattern; hiring freezes and market shifts confound it.
