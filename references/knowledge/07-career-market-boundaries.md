# Market and evidence boundaries

Fresh sources are needed for live jobs, hiring signals, companies, salary ranges,
technology adoption or policy. Record URL, date checked and exact scope. The engine
ships no job feed or company facts and never converts cached advice into current truth.

Separate technology enthusiasm from demonstrated demand; one public job post is
not a market. Do not send personal resumes, contact details, salaries or private
repository contents to search engines. Use generic queries and authorized public
sources. Private material remains local unless the user expressly authorizes disclosure.

When browsing is unavailable, say which market assumptions remain unverified and
give a source-check action. A legal or financial issue can require current professional
information; a deterministic score cannot resolve it.
