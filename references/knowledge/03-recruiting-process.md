# Recruiting signals

Record application stage, last contact, promised date, actual elapsed working days,
next action, and deadline. “Three days” may include weekends or holidays; ask only
when the distinction changes timing. Do not claim to know local holidays offline.

Silence has multiple explanations: scheduling, comparisons, approval or low priority.
None establishes rejection. Short answers at the end of an interview also cannot
reveal a verdict. An explicit rejection settles this application, not personal worth
or every role of that type. “HC frozen” is a reported employer explanation, not a
diagnosis of the candidate's skills.

Use the promised date first. If none exists, one short follow-up after about three
working days is an adjustable heuristic. After another comparable interval, lower
investment and keep alternatives moving. Urgent competing offers change the timing.
If asked not to contact again, stop. Never invent another offer or a deadline.
