# Evidence and claims

Before improving wording, ask what the exact sentence promises and who did what.
Use `claim`, `confidence`, `evidence`, `ownership`, `risk`, plus project stage.

| Level | What it means | Allowed expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VERIFIED | Operator checked direct evidence covering this exact claim | The narrow checked result with scope and attribution |
| SUPPORTED | Relevant supporting artifacts; not independently checked in full | A bounded result with source limitation |
| SELF_REPORTED | Candidate account only, or artifact unrelated to the assertion | “据本人描述…”; no new precision or stronger ownership |
| PLANNED | Unfinished goal | “计划 / 进行中”; never completed |

A URL alone proves nothing. A repo can show code but not personal authorship;
commit metadata may need user identity confirmation. Demo deployment does not
prove production traffic. A benchmark needs baseline, sample, conditions and
measurement; a backtest needs fees, slippage, leakage and out-of-sample limits.
Awards need official results matching participant identity and actual award.

For “主导”, inspect owned decisions, actual work, teammates' work, AI/tool work,
review authority and alternative choices. If unclear, write “参与团队项目，负责
[已确认部分]” without filling the placeholder with a guess. “主导” is not “独立完成”.
An automated text trigger is only a prompt to audit; manually inspect ambiguous
or unsupported technologies, metrics, revenue and titles too.

Split compound statements: product leadership does not verify a neighboring claim
of architecture design, implementation or deployment authorship. Keep reported
product decisions and coordination while narrowing unreceipted engineering credit.
The local helper recognizes limited creation clauses; coordination is not personal
implementation. It cannot resolve every paraphrase, attribution or mixed clause.

Normalize before writing or storing: `python scripts/junshi.py audit --input file.json`.
The helper consumes a structured operator record; it does not fetch evidence or
authenticate a fabricated verification receipt. Evidence verification remains a
real host task. Planned work cannot be upgraded by a requested confidence label.
