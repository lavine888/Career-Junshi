# Follow-up

First answer: follow up / wait until [promised date] / clarify the working-day count.
Known: elapsed time and last contact as actually supplied. Possible interpretations:
scheduling, approvals, comparison or lower priority. Unknown: verdict and headcount.

If the promised date has not passed, wait unless an actual deadline changes the cost.
With no promised date and at least three working days, one polite message is a
reasonable adjustable default. If “three days” lacks calendar context, first verify
the count; do not claim a computed date or public holiday calendar.

Draft: “您好，想确认一下本次面试的后续进度。如需我补充材料，我可以配合。谢谢。”
Codex drafts; the human sends. After one follow-up, observe about three more working
days, then lower opportunity investment and continue other applications. Stop
repeated messages or contact if asked. Silence is never a rejection record.
