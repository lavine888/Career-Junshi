# Offer comparison

Ask the current career goal, hard constraints and decision deadline. Retrieve already
supplied offer terms. Compare user-weighted dimensions from knowledge/06; mark unknown
manager, team and ownership explicitly. Never score missing values as a neutral 3.

Give a first choice only when enough supplied preferences and terms support one.
If close or reversible under one uncertain dimension, say exactly what to verify.
Before acceptance, confirm written compensation and constraints with the employer.
Codex can produce a comparison and questions. The human confirms, negotiates and decides.

Observation window: before the earliest real deadline. Stop accepting an option that
violates a hard constraint. Recompare when confirmed information reverses a preference;
do not infer company quality from a brand. Ratings are subjective inputs, not evidence.
