# Compensation alignment and post-interview negotiation

Use for salary expectations, post-interview compensation questions, offer
negotiation or a requested rehearsal. Reuse the current opportunity and prior
expectations from this chat or a supplied continuation card; no automatic history
search. This practical replaces an unrelated resource within the 1–3 budget.

## Establish the actual stage

| Available information | Useful next move |
| --- | --- |
| Interview finished; HR asks expectations; no offer | Align role scope and compensation basis, prepare an honest response |
| Informal range or verbal proposal | Clarify what is proposed, conditional or still awaiting approval |
| Written offer or explicit counterproposal | Compare actual terms against user priorities, then make one focused request |

Do not refuse early salary coaching because an offer is absent. Conversely,
interview completion, several rounds or HR asking a number do not prove an offer,
a strong bargaining position or a guaranteed budget increase.

Reuse supplied location/currency, level/scope, compensation breakdown, user's
desired outcome, alternatives and actual reply deadline. Ask only the 1–3 missing
items that could change the next move; a short script need not await every field.
Keep the user's private walk-away point distinct from the range they choose to
disclose. Current salary, competing offers and acceptance intent must not be
invented or demanded as compulsory inputs.

## Compare like terms before bargaining

Keep monthly base, explicitly guaranteed pay periods, variable bonus, one-time
signing payments, benefits and equity separate. Note currency, tax basis and
first-year versus recurring amounts. Do not assume an extra month's salary,
bonus payout, equity liquidity or future promotion is guaranteed.

Compute only from known terms, show the basis, and leave unknown components
unpriced. Do not add an unvested grant to annual guaranteed cash, silently convert
currencies or treat an employer's notional equity value as realized income.
Any exchange rate used needs its source/date and stated uncertainty.

If a requested number or market comparison needs external support, verify fresh
sources for the actual role/level/location and compensation basis. Published job
bands or a supplied employer range support their stated scope; self-reported
anecdotes alone do not establish this employer's band. Without comparable data,
draft a request for the range or a conditional script, not a fabricated benchmark.
No fixed percentage increase or universal first-number rule.

## Prepare one ask and rehearse the actual response

Choose the one or two terms that matter most to this user. Produce a concise
ready-to-review draft: interest → specific request → honest basis → a fallback
or request to clarify flexibility. Use placeholders where the user has not
selected a number. Do not expose the private minimum in the outgoing draft or
pretend an alternative is a competing offer.

For a quick expectation question before an offer, a useful starting draft is:
“我对这个岗位有兴趣。想先确认薪酬结构和预算口径，方便我结合职责范围给出一致的预期。”
Adapt to what HR already asked and what the user has already disclosed; do not
keep deflecting a question that now needs an answer.

If rehearsal is requested, explicitly play HR and ask one question at a time,
using supplied terms. Base a pushback on those terms, or label an invented limit
as a hypothetical training condition. After the user's attempt, identify one
useful move and one adjustment; do not announce that a concession was obtained.
A rehearsal request does not authorize sending messages.

## Return actual terms to the same opportunity

The human sends, negotiates, accepts or rejects. Update the comparison only from
an actual reported counterproposal or confirmed term, preserving its source,
date and provisional/written status. A draft request remains unsent; an approval
to seek budget is not an agreed increase. Choose a follow-up window from the
actual promise or deadline, not a made-up urgency tactic.

Stop at the user's walk-away boundary, explicit refusal, time limit or request
to stop. Recommend one next confirmation or alternative rather than endless
counteroffers. Reuse the existing journey/offer record when supplied; no new
coaching-state file or automatic memory.

For decisive term checks, use [offer-check](offer-check.md) in place of an
unnecessary reference. Contract, tax or immigration questions require current
authoritative sources for the jurisdiction; do not infer legal protection or
unsupported take-home pay. Research and practice are not salary guarantees.
