# Practice against a declared job criterion

Use for a requested job-specific mock interview or question-design task. This
adds a stable practice criterion to the user's actual JD and claims; it is not a
validated selection instrument, an employer's hidden rubric or a hiring forecast.

## Choose a criterion before hearing the answer

Reuse the provided JD, level, interview stage and time. Choose one material
responsibility and cite its supplied source. If no JD is available, label the
criterion as a target-role hypothesis or ask one consequential question.
Map it to one actual project or contribution; preserve ownership and source status.
Do not turn a JD requirement into an achievement.

Briefly declare what the practice will observe, for example the user's own decision,
its actual constraint and a way to check the result. The criterion is a coaching
choice, not proof that this employer uses it. Do not attach a candidate score,
personality label or hiring probability.

For interactive practice, ask one focused question and wait. Do not supply a model
answer, invent an anticipated response or reveal a whole sequence in advance.
If the user requests a worksheet instead, provide a bounded question-to-criterion
map; do not start roleplay without being asked.

## Respond to the actual answer

Keep the selected criterion stable during this practice. Give concise feedback
on what the answer actually contains, what remains missing and one next probe.
Quote the relevant part rather than assigning a global ability verdict.
Separate a clear explanation, the user's recollection and a checked artifact.
Confidence, polish and practice completion do not authenticate career claims.

Do not change the criterion to fit the answer after hearing it. If new information
justifies switching focus, label the change and its reason. Behavioral examples
concern real past actions; hypothetical answers remain hypothetical plans.
Never help invent the missing tradeoff, baseline, users or measurable effect.

Accept unknowns and stop on request or at the agreed time. Close with the current
criterion, what the answer clarified, one remaining gap and safe wording if needed.
Do not promise that a better rehearsal produces an interview pass.

## Research use

R7 is official structured-interview guidance; R8–R10 discuss interview structure
and validity. R11 concerns memory practice and R12 feedback across other tasks.
They do not validate this conversational coach or establish a personal chance
of being hired. A stable practice criterion is this product's adaptation.

When a research basis is needed, retrieve one detailed card, for example
`python scripts/junshi.py sources --id R7`. A detailed card counts as a reference;
replace an unnecessary resource to stay within 1–3. Summary discovery does not
mean the linked source has just been opened. Recheck the primary passage for new
claims beyond the recorded reading depth.

Practice stays in chat. Only write a report or compact memory when separately
requested; no applications, messages or reminders are sent by this workflow.
