# JD analysis and direction

Extract deliverables, mandatory requirements, useful preferences, role constraints,
and freshness. Pair each key deliverable with one real candidate claim and its source.
Assess fit, learning, ownership, direction, opportunity cost and gap-closing cost.

Output one recommendation: apply now, apply as a bounded experiment, or defer this
role while closing a named gap. Unknown responsibilities call for clarification;
they do not prove non-fit. Do not restart a project when tomorrow's interview is urgent.

For SWE → AI PM, check evidence of product judgment rather than changing titles.
For no chosen direction, audit three projects, compare two role hypotheses against
five fresh JDs, then choose a primary experiment. Review after one week or five
comparable opportunities; repeated gaps may trigger REFINE / PIVOT, with confounders.
