# JD analysis and direction

Extract deliverables, mandatory requirements, useful preferences, constraints and
freshness from the supplied JD. Preserve the actual clause and source locator;
merge repeated requirements and exclude company marketing. A role name alone
does not establish its requirements. Without a JD, give a bounded direction
hypothesis rather than a verified match or current-vacancy verdict.

## Separate the obstacle from the next action

Pair each decisive requirement with an existing candidate claim, source,
confidence and personal contribution. Classify the current record, not the whole
candidate. Missing keywords do not establish missing ability; adjacent technologies
do not establish experience with the requested stack.

| Current state | Basis | Useful next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing support / 已有支持 | Relevant, attributable material supports this requirement within its scope | Retain the claim and prepare its defense; keep source confidence |
| Expression gap / 表达缺口 | Supplied facts support the work but the resume omits or obscures it | Rewrite from those same facts |
| Insufficient evidence / 证据不足 | A claim or hint exists, but scope, authorship or outcome lacks support | Inspect existing material or design one bounded evidence task |
| Actual gap / 真实缺口 | The user confirms not having done it, or supplied facts explicitly fail the requirement | Plan relevant practice, choose a bounded application experiment or defer |
| Unknown / 待确认 | Decisive information is absent or conflicting | Ask one consequential question or retain the unknown |

When several problems coexist, state the decisive one and its reason. Polishing
cannot resolve missing evidence or capability. A reported accomplishment can
remain self-reported even when relevant; “existing support” is not VERIFIED.

Show hard constraints separately: location, work authorization, level, required
qualification or certificate, when the JD actually makes them mandatory. Strong
projects cannot cancel an unmet hard gate. “Preferred / familiar / a plus” does
not become mandatory, and an unknown gate does not become a confirmed failure.
Check fresh external sources when the decision depends on a vacancy or policy
still being current; a supplied historic JD only establishes that document.

## Deliver one decision with a return path

Lead with apply now, apply as a bounded experiment, clarify first, or defer while
closing a named gap. Give the reason, not a match percentage. Use a compact matrix:
requirement / importance and source → candidate evidence and boundary → current
state → next action. Show decisive rows rather than an exhaustive keyword checklist.

Choose at most 1–3 priority actions, considering fit, learning, ownership, direction,
opportunity cost, the user's budget and deadline. Use existing work first:
[resume](resume.md) for expression, [evidence sprint](evidence-sprint.md) for missing
support, or [structured interview](structured-interview.md) for a requested rehearsal.
Replace an unnecessary reference; all selected resources still total 1–3.
A classification alone does not send an application or authorize unrelated work.
Continue edits or preparation already requested by the user.

For each action, name what to bring back and what it could change: a sourced rewrite,
an inspected artifact, a clarified constraint or an observed answer. Practice and
new work remain planned until performed; they do not repair historical claims
retroactively. For a continuing search, carry the decisive requirement, source,
resume/claim version and next action into the existing journey card, not a second
tracker. Unknown responsibilities do not prove non-fit.

For SWE → AI PM, check actual product judgment rather than changing titles.
For no chosen direction, compare supplied projects and relevant role hypotheses
against fresh JDs as time permits, then choose one primary experiment. A one-week
or five-comparable-opportunity review is an adjustable planning window; repeated
gaps may support REFINE / PIVOT only with alternatives and confounders checked.
Do not restart a project when tomorrow's interview calls for existing evidence.
