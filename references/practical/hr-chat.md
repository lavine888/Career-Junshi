# HR chat and founder outreach

Separate visible messages and dates from candidate interpretation. Draft one short
message with one purpose. Respect a no-contact instruction. Do not send messages.

Follow-up draft: “您好，想确认一下本次面试的后续进度。如需我补充材料，我可以配合。
谢谢。” Insert only confirmed role / interview details. If there is a real competing
deadline, disclose its actual date rather than inventing urgency.

Founder outreach: identify a specific public problem, link one honest relevant artifact,
and make one small request. No invented familiarity, users or company endorsement.
The user sends it. Observe replies; after one unanswered follow-up, lower investment.
