# Job search experiment

Turn one recruiting uncertainty into a bounded learning plan, then review actual
observations. This is a host-led workflow, not an application sender or an RCT.
Deliver the experiment card in chat. Write only to a user-requested private path;
never send applications, collect a full personal history or enable memory by default.

## Establish the question

Reuse provided records and constraints. Separate completed applications from plans,
verified artifacts from user reports, and observations from explanations. Ask only
for missing facts that change the plan. Do not infer a causal improvement from two
historical batches with different roles, channels, dates or observation windows.

Choose one question and one explicitly uncertain hypothesis. Define the smallest
useful comparison and a decision it could inform. A process improvement, such as
reducing preparation time, may be more assessable than a hiring effect. If research
reasoning is needed, load [research-and-application.md](../knowledge/08-research-and-application.md)
within the total 1–3 reference budget. Sources support reasoning principles, not a
promised response rate or an individualized hiring prediction.

## Freeze a feasible experiment card

- Question, hypothesis, current evidence and known alternative explanations.
- Baseline resume / material identifiers, provided sources, ownership and stage.
  Preserve original wording; record exactly one permitted wording or relevance
  change. No fabricated metric, upgraded ownership or hidden extra edits.
- A usable baseline and usable variant. Never deliberately send an inferior,
  inaccurate or unsuitable resume merely to create a control group.
- Eligible role family, seniority, must-have qualifications, channel and dates;
  exclusions and unavailable information. Similarity is a constraint, not proof
  that employers or applicant pools are equivalent.
- Assignment / matching rule and unavoidable confounders. Versions sent to
  different employers do not establish randomization or causal identification.
- Adjustable target of unique opportunities, preparation / review time caps,
  timezone, application cutoff and observation cutoff. Label all unperformed
  actions planned. No default sample size or significance guarantee.
- Funnel definitions and denominator for each rate: unique applications,
  substantive replies, interviews and offers remain distinct stages. Exclude
  automated acknowledgments from substantive replies; show stage counts explicitly.
- One defined observation window from actual submission time; keep immature
  observations pending. Silence at the window cutoff is no observed reply within
  that window, not a verified rejection. Define later replies separately.
- Continue / revise / stop criteria tied to budget and usable information,
  including permission to stop with insufficient evidence. Do not move a recorded
  cutoff after seeing outcomes without labeling a new plan.

Use the user's real budget. Narrow scope if the work does not fit; do not make
future applications sound completed or require expansion to obtain a clear winner.
The card records a learning plan, not consent to send or save anything.

## Review the returned observations

Keep the frozen card visible and distinguish its original rules from amendments.
Deduplicate by opportunity identity (company, role / requisition and recruiting
process), not by message count. Merge status updates on one opportunity without
inventing applications; preserve conflicting records as unresolved. A reply,
interview invitation and follow-up for one application are not three samples.

Report planned versus actually submitted, eligible versus excluded / unresolved,
and mature versus pending observations for each version. Apply the same window to
comparable submissions; state mature denominators, counts and missing data. Avoid
aggregate rates that silently mix maturity, stage or noncomparable historical data.

Compare observations with the recorded hypothesis and alternative explanations.
Small, nonrandom comparisons may guide a cautious next action; they do not prove
causality, statistical significance, a population effect or a candidate's ability.
An offer never retroactively validates unsupported resume wording. Choose continue,
revise or stop with reasons, actual remaining budget and unresolved uncertainty.
Give one next action; stopping without a winner is a valid result. Keep material
sources and original Claim boundaries intact throughout.
