The largest decision-quality gaps: raw-material interpretation cannot be checked
against spans; alternatives are generic rather than case-specific; no reconsider
contract; no task-relevant historical retrieval; predictions are not compared with
observations; no blinded golden-case packet / dimension rubric. Resume/JD preference,
team authorship and subjective interview impressions need extraction regressions.

Non-goals: rewrite working v0.1, add modes, services, UI, databases, knowledge encyclopedia
or probability claims. The five planners, SQLite tables, licenses, installation model,
private-data boundary and all original 61 tests remain the compatibility baseline.
Tests do not certify model reasoning or real hiring effects. Real outcomes will never
be manufactured to make a public benchmark appear more mature.
