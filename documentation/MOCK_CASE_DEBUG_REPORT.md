## Cross-case Failure Clusters

1. Document presence versus empty structured Claims: decision/artifact defect.
2. Product leadership versus engineering authorship: confidence guard defect.
3. Unknown scope versus accomplishment assertion: validator defect.
4. Measurement: omitted nested contracts, lossy repair, mode/date/no-contact errors, excessive UNKNOWN and stage ambiguity.
5. Decision defaults: risk-count ranking/templates underpersonalize; direction lacks prioritization and Offer overpauses.
6. Host variance: fragmented tasks, unsupported positioning specifics and missing question ladder. One paired sample cannot prove stable improvement/regression.
