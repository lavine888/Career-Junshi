## 最可能改变选择的未知（最多三项）

- B's written role scope
  - 为什么：The founder's broad verbal description does not establish actual product leadership, engineering authorship, or customer responsibility.
  - 核实：Request written responsibilities, decision rights, engineering expectations, customer duties, and ownership boundaries.
  - 反转：Written scope materially narrows or contradicts the founder's verbal statement.
- B's runway
  - 为什么：Runway is explicitly missing and affects the downside of joining a 25-person startup.
  - 核实：Ask for the current runway basis and the assumptions used to calculate it.
  - 反转：Verified runway changes the startup downside assessment materially.
- Manager quality for both offers
  - 为什么：Both offer records mark manager quality as unknown, and the user values growth and future optionality.
  - 核实：Speak with each direct manager about feedback cadence, decision rights, expectations, and examples of team-member growth.
  - 反转：A material manager-quality difference is established between the options.

## 反转条件

- Written scope materially narrows or contradicts the founder's verbal statement.
- Verified runway changes the startup downside assessment materially.
- A material manager-quality difference is established between the options.
